Burnt honey& black fig tart

Honey taken almost too far, until it smells of toasted bread and smoke, then whisked into a brown-butter custard and crowded with halved figs. Bitter, floral, barely sweet.

Ingredients 8

    Method tap a step to start its timer

    1. Blind-bake the pastry case at 190 °C for 18 minutes, then 6 more without weights.
      18:00
    2. Cook the honey in a wide pan until it darkens to mahogany and just begins to smoke. Off the heat, whisk in the butter.
      6:00
    3. Whisk eggs, cream and salt; stream in the warm honey butter.
      —
    4. Pour into the case, press in the figs cut-side up, and bake at 160 °C for 35–40 minutes, until barely set.
      38:00
    5. Cool for an hour. Finish with flaky salt and a little thyme.
      60:00

    The honey is ready when a wisp of smoke rises and it smells like the crust of a dark loaf. Ten seconds more and it turns acrid — have the butter cubed and waiting.
